Original composition by Penbu for piano + violin duet, inspired by a private [piano improv session][piano-improv] and developed [live on stream][making-of playlist].

[piano-improv]: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JHBPa-tNtJc
[making-of playlist]: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLNMaWutZ8vfMTKn-F_SYzCtV8IJupMOsM

This piece was preformed by Penbu and [Syzygy Altair][] on the piano and violin respectively. The art was drawn by [Mino][] with coloured pencils on paper.

[Syzygy Altair]: https://www.youtube.com/@SyzygyAltairCh
[Mino]: https://www.instagram.com/rmino_36

The score was created with [MuseScore 3](https://musescore.org/) and rendered with Muse Sounds.

Comments from Mino:

> Hi everyone! I’m really excited about this release. Penbu and Z worked hard to make it happen, and I truly hope you enjoy and appreciate it as much as I do! I want to take this opportunity to thank them both for the chance they gave me and—since this is Z’s last livestream before her indefinite break—I want to thank her for everything she’s done for us over the past two years, wishing her the best and hoping she’ll return someday :)
> 
> Thanks everyone, and... enjoy!
